Step-By-Step Journey

How You Learn Driving With Us

Our certified curriculum takes you from an absolute beginner to a confident, licensed driver through systematic practical stages.

1

Admission & LL

Register online or visit our Ghoshpukur office. We prepare your Learner License application on Parivahan Sarathi.

2

Dual Control Basics

Get comfortable with vehicle controls, steering posture, clutch-accelerator bite point in our dual-brake safety cars.

3

Traffic & Highway

Advance to real road driving on Ghoshpukur town roads and NH 31 highway with overtaking and lane change discipline.

4

Track Drills & Parking

Master parallel parking, reverse S-bend, and slope starting on our dedicated training ground poles.

5

RTO Trial & License

We accompany you to the Siliguri RTO for your 8-shape & H-track driving test and ensure your permanent DL issuance.

Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries candidates have before joining Ghoshpukur Motor Training School.

What is the minimum age to apply for motor driving training?
For gearless two-wheelers (up to 50cc), the minimum age is 16 years (with parental consent). For standard light motor vehicles (Cars, Geared Motorcycles), the minimum age required by the Motor Vehicles Act is 18 years.
What documents are required for admission and Learner's License?
You will need: (1) Aadhaar Card or Voter ID, (2) Age proof (Birth Certificate or 10th Admit Card / PAN), (3) 3 passport-sized color photographs, and (4) Medical Certificate Form 1-A (for candidates above 40 years). Our school assists with all documentation.
Are the training cars safe for absolute beginners?
Yes, 100%! All our training hatchbacks (Swift, WagonR, Alto) are government-certified dual-control vehicles equipped with secondary clutch and brake pedals on the instructor's passenger side, ensuring complete safety at all times.
Can I choose my own preferred driving time slot?
Yes, we provide flexible training hours from 6:00 AM to 7:00 PM every day including Sundays. You can select an early morning, late afternoon, or weekend batch according to your office, college, or business schedule.
Do you offer ladies-only batches with female driving instructors?
Yes, Ghoshpukur Motor Training School has certified lady trainers and exclusive batches for women learners to ensure a comfortable, supportive, and stress-free driving experience.
