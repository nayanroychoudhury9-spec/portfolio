# portfolio

## Nayan Roy Choudhury - Executive Portfolio & Resume Website

A modern, responsive, animated personal portfolio and digital resume website showcasing the professional career of **Nayan Roy Choudhury** — Real Estate Sales Executive, Industrial Factory Dispatch Head, and Former International Maritime Professional based in Siliguri, West Bengal, India.

---

### 🌟 Key Highlights & Sections

- **Hero & Interactive 3D Card**: Live typewriter effect highlighting career roles, floating badges (*Mjb Group*, *Sanyasihat Tea*, *Maral Dariya Marine*), and 3D mouse-tilt avatar holding candidate photo.
- **Dynamic Stats Bar**: Animated count-up metrics for 7+ years of multi-industry experience, dispatch reliability, and languages.
- **Filterable Experience Timeline**:
  - **Current Role**: Dispatch Head – Tea Factory (*Sanyasihat Tea Processing Pvt. Ltd., Mjb Group*)
  - **Real Estate Executive**: *Mjb Group – Project: Embee Delight, Siliguri*
  - **B.P.O Specialist**: *P.P Enterprise, Webel I.T Park, Siliguri*
  - **Engine Rating (GP Rating)**: *Maral Dariya Commercial Vessel, Iran*
- **Skill Competency Meters**: Sales & negotiation, factory logistics & stock control, tax invoices & e-way bills, and professional leadership.
- **Language Proficiency Gauges**: Bengali (Native 100%), Hindi (C2 Proficient 98%), and English (A2 Elementary 65%).
- **Academic & Maritime Credentials**: Global Maritime Academy Dehradun (GP Rating Diploma) & Margaret S.N School.
- **Direct Contact & Quick Action Dock**: One-click phone calling (`+91 8768081380`), direct WhatsApp messaging, email client trigger, and full CV PDF download.
- **Theme Switcher**: Dark Luxury Slate and Modern Clean Light themes.

---

### 🚀 Tech Stack

- **HTML5**: Semantic, accessible markup.
- **CSS3**: Custom CSS variables, glassmorphism (`backdrop-filter`), CSS Grid & Flexbox, smooth transitions, print stylesheet.
- **JavaScript (ES6+)**: Vanilla JS, IntersectionObserver scroll-trigger animations, typed text rotation, 3D mouse parallax.
- **Icons & Fonts**: Font Awesome 6, Google Fonts (*Outfit* and *Plus Jakarta Sans*).
